# Research and evidence limits
- Nielsen Norman Group, concise/scannable/objective: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/
- NN/g, how users read: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-users-read-on-the-web/
- Baymard, homepage and navigation: https://baymard.com/research/homepage-and-category-usability
- W3C WCAG 2.2: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- W3C overview: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/glance/
- web.dev performance: https://web.dev/articles/vitals
- Claude Code skills: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
- Codex skills: https://developers.openai.com/codex/skills

No research establishes a universal ideal landing-page length or that halving copy improves conversion. Baymard primarily covers ecommerce. Accessibility requires testing. Conversion lift requires experiments.
