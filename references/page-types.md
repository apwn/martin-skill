# Page-type guardrails
Structure first on every type. Map sections (job, height in screens, visual weight) before cutting words. A short word count can still hide a tall, repetitive scroll.

- Tiny SaaS: short pitch and demo; preserve pricing and limits. Watch for the same pitch restated in hero, feature strip, FAQ and a closing banner.
- B2B SaaS: compress repetition; preserve security, integrations, procurement objections. Don't put a logo wall before the offer, or bury the CTA under six feature grids.
- Ecommerce: simplify decoration; preserve categories, search, shipping, returns. Collapse duplicate promo banners, not the catalog.
- Developer tool: simplify marketing; preserve API constraints, examples and prerequisites. Keep compatibility on the page or one click away, not deleted.
- Regulated offer: preserve warnings, disclosures and eligibility. Structure around the offer, never hide required copy.
- Portfolio: preserve proof of work and contact details. One proof section beats four restated bios.
- Editorial/SEO: preserve substantive answers and sources. Merge repeated explainers; don't flatten a useful outline into one blob.
- Multilingual: preserve translation coverage and consistent meaning. Apply the same outline in every locale.
